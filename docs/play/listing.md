## Short description (80 chars max)

## Full description (4000 chars max)

**The first app you won't use.**

Set WiM up once, then close it. A small floating bubble stays on top of
whatever you're doing. That bubble IS the app.

**Tap — talk — tap again. Done.**
WiM listens, cleans up what you said, and types the finished text straight
into the app you're in — Messages, Gmail, WhatsApp, Telegram, anywhere you
can type, you can talk. When the bubble turns green, one tap sends it.

**Other voice apps race to type your exact words. We don't.**
Give WiM your messy, real, thinking-out-loud speech — the "um"s, the
restarts, the sentence you changed halfway — and it hands back the message
you meant to write. Like handing a rough draft to an editor. Your words,
your meaning, tidied up. Casual or Professional — your pick.

**Built for people the other apps gave up on.**
WiM was built by a speaker with a stutter, for everyone whose speech
doesn't come out in a straight line: people who stutter or block,
non-native speakers, fast talkers, tired brains. Repetitions are bridged.
False starts are cleaned. What you meant survives.
WiM is an assistive dictation tool, not a medical device.

**Four gears.**
- Transcribe (free, on your phone): your words as spoken, tidied — no
  cloud, no account, ever.
- Reconstruct: cloud cleanup that rewrites messy speech into clean text.
- Profile: same, plus your own vocabulary and word fixes.
- Speech Disfluency: the deep gear — built around how disfluent speech
  actually works.

**Speak your language.**
Full dictation in English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Russian, German,
Italian, Hindi, Arabic, Japanese, Korean, and Chinese. Five of those
translate the app's menus too.

**Private by design.**
Your recordings live on your phone only while a take is processed, then
they're deleted. Free mode never touches the internet. No ads. No selling
your data. Full policy: https://gugosf114.github.io/wim-android/privacy.html

**WiM Cloud** unlocks the cloud gears: $9.99/month, 300 cloud takes,
up to 20 deep-gear takes. The free gear is free forever.

Speak like yourself. Send like you meant it.

# Data safety form — answers

**Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?** Yes.

**Audio (voice or sound recordings)**
- Collected? Yes (sent off device on cloud modes for speech-to-text and
  cleanup). Free local mode sends nothing.
- Shared? With service providers only (OpenAI / Anthropic) to provide the
  feature. Not for advertising. Not sold.
- Processed ephemerally? Yes — processed then discarded; not stored by us.
- Required? No — user can stay on the free local mode.
- Purpose: App functionality.

**Personal info — email address**
- Collected? Only if the user signs in (optional).
- Purpose: Account management, settings sync.
- Shared? No.

**Messages / other user content (dictation text)**
- Stored on device by default (not "collected"). If the user signs in and
  enables cloud backup: collected, purpose app functionality, user-deletable.

**Security practices**
- Data encrypted in transit: Yes.
- Users can request deletion: Yes — in-app "Delete my cloud account and
  data" (immediate) and email.

**Data deletion path:** in-app button + email gugosf@gmail.com.
