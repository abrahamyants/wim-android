WiM — What I Meant: Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 6, 2026

WiM is an assistive dictation tool. You speak; WiM turns your speech into clean text and types it into the app you are using. This page says, in plain words, what data WiM touches and what happens to it.

Your voice

When you record, the sound is held on your phone only while your take is being processed, then deleted. WiM keeps no library of your recordings.

On the free local mode, your voice never leaves your phone.

On cloud modes, the audio of your take is sent over an encrypted connection to our speech providers (OpenAI) to be turned into text, and the text may be sent to a language-model provider (OpenAI or Anthropic) to be cleaned up. Under the providers' API terms, this data is not used to train their models. We do not store your audio on our servers.

Your text

Your dictation history (the text results) is stored on your phone. You can delete any session, or all of them, in the app. If you sign in and turn on cloud backup, session text is also stored in your account's cloud space until you delete it.

Your account

Signing in with Google is optional. If you sign in, we store your email and your profile settings (your vocabulary, corrections, and preferences) so they can sync between your devices. The app includes "Export my data" and "Delete my cloud account and data" buttons that do exactly what they say — deletion is immediate and permanent.

What WiM does not do

  • No ads, and no ad tracking.
  • No sale of your data, to anyone, ever.
  • No reading of your screen beyond what is needed to type text into the field you chose and, if you enable it, to find that app's send button.
  • No medical claims. WiM is an assistive dictation tool, not a medical device.

Permissions, in plain words

  • Microphone — to hear you when you record.
  • Display over other apps — the floating bubble.
  • Accessibility — to type the finished text into the app you are using. WiM uses this power only to write your text and, if you turn on auto-send, to tap send for you.
  • Notifications — the small tray entry that brings the bubble back.

Children

WiM is not directed at children under 13.
