# Google Play Health apps declaration — WiM

- Select: **My app doesn't provide any health features.**

Reason: WiM is an assistive voice-typing and productivity utility. It transcribes user-requested speech and inserts text into a focused field. It does not monitor health, provide therapy, diagnose a condition, recommend treatment, connect users with clinicians, or make clinical decisions.

Keep this disclaimer in the store description and in-app guide:

> WiM is an assistive dictation tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Do not describe Layer 4 as "clinical mode" in user-facing copy. Its release name is **Speech Disfluency** or **deep reconstruction**.
