# WiM: Empowering Voice-to-Text for Everyone

## What is WiM?
WiM is a completely private, offline voice-to-text application engineered specifically to handle the nuances of human speech, including stuttering and pauses. Unlike traditional dictation apps that rely on constant internet connections and often fail to accurately transcribe non-fluent speech, WiM runs entirely on your device. It takes your spoken words, accurately transcribes them locally, and formats them into clean, polished text ready to be pasted into your favorite messaging, email, or notes apps. 

## Who It's For
WiM was built from the ground up for **people who stutter**, their Speech-Language Pathologists (SLPs), and anyone who finds standard voice-to-text tools frustrating, inaccurate, or intrusive. 
If you have ever felt rushed by a microphone timing out, or frustrated by an app that stops listening the moment you pause to breathe or block, WiM is for you. It's also perfect for accessibility-focused users and privacy advocates who want powerful voice dictation without sacrificing their personal data to big tech clouds.

## How It Works
WiM integrates smoothly into your daily life. A convenient floating bubble sits on your screen, ready whenever you need it. Simply tap the bubble while in any texting or typing app, speak at your own pace, and watch as WiM transcribes your voice in real-time. 

Behind the scenes, WiM uses cutting-edge, lightweight local AI models (like Vosk and Moonshine) to process your speech right on your phone. Once you're done speaking, WiM reconstructs the transcript, removing repetitions and fixing formatting to deliver clean, highly accurate text that you can insert anywhere with a single tap.

## Uncompromising Privacy Guarantees
Your voice is deeply personal, and it belongs to you. **WiM is offline-first by design.** 
- **No Cloud Required:** The core voice-to-text processing happens entirely on your device's hardware. Your audio recordings are never sent to a remote server for transcription unless you explicitly opt-in to optional cloud features.
- **Your Data, Your Rules:** We don't harvest your voice data to train our models. 
- **Optional Sync:** For users who want cross-device syncing, we offer optional, secure cloud backup, but the app is fully functional without it. 

You get the power of advanced speech recognition with the security of a closed ecosystem.

## Languages Supported
Communication is global. WiM currently supports on-device, offline transcription for 10 languages, allowing you to dictate comfortably in the language you know best.
