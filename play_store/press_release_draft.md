# Press Release Draft: WiM Launch

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

## WiM Launches Offline-First Voice-to-Text App Built Specifically for People Who Stutter
**New Android application brings private, highly accurate, and accessible dictation directly to your device—no cloud required.**

**[CITY, STATE] – [Date]** – Today marks the launch of WiM, a revolutionary new voice-to-text application for Android designed to solve a persistent problem: standard dictation tools fail people who stutter. By combining on-device AI processing with a user experience tailored for non-fluent speech, WiM offers a private, stress-free way to turn voice into polished text.

For the millions of individuals globally who stutter, modern voice assistants and dictation apps are often sources of frustration. These cloud-reliant tools frequently time out during pauses, misinterpret repetitions, or abruptly stop listening. WiM changes this paradigm. Built from the ground up with the stuttering community in mind, WiM never rushes the user. It utilizes lightweight, state-of-the-art local AI models like Vosk and Moonshine to process speech entirely offline, ensuring that the microphone stays open as long as the user needs it to.

"Most commercial voice-to-text engines assume perfect fluency and a rapid internet connection," said the creator of WiM. "We wanted to build something that respects the natural cadence of a person's voice, regardless of pauses or blocks. WiM reconstructs the transcript locally, removing repetitions and formatting the text cleanly, so the user can communicate exactly what they intend to, completely stress-free."

Beyond accessibility, WiM is a massive step forward for digital privacy. Because the app operates offline-first, a user’s voice data never leaves their smartphone unless they explicitly opt into optional cloud syncing. This makes WiM an ideal tool not just for people who stutter, but for professionals handling sensitive information, privacy advocates, and users in areas with poor internet connectivity. The app features a convenient floating bubble that allows users to dictate directly into their favorite messaging, email, or notes applications seamlessly.

WiM currently supports 10 languages and is available globally on the Google Play Store. The app is free to download for basic offline transcription, with a one-time unlock of $3.99 for premium features such as advanced engine toggles and custom formatting—ensuring that this crucial accessibility tool remains affordable without relying on predatory subscription models.

**About WiM**
WiM is an independent software project dedicated to creating accessible, privacy-first communication tools. Focused on empowering users through on-device processing and thoughtful design, WiM aims to make technology adapt to the human voice, not the other way around.
