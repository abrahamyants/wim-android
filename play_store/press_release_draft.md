# Press Release Draft: WiM Launch

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

## WiM Launches Offline-First Voice-to-Text App Built Specifically for People with Speech Disfluencies
**New Android application brings private, highly accurate, and accessible dictation directly to your device—no cloud required.**

**Daly City, CA – May 29, 2026** – Today marks the launch of WiM, a new voice-to-text application for Android designed to solve a persistent problem: standard dictation tools fail people with speech disfluencies. By combining on-device speech processing with a user experience tailored for non-fluent speech, WiM offers a private, stress-free way to turn voice into polished text.

For the millions of individuals globally with speech disfluencies, modern voice assistants and dictation apps are often sources of frustration. These cloud-reliant tools frequently time out during pauses, misinterpret repetitions, or abruptly stop listening. WiM changes this paradigm. Built from the ground up with the speech-disfluency community in mind, WiM never rushes the user. By default, it runs a lightweight on-device speech model (whisper-tiny.en, ~40 MB) to transcribe entirely offline, keeping the microphone open as long as the user needs it.

"Most commercial voice-to-text engines assume perfect fluency and a rapid internet connection," said the creator of WiM. "We wanted to build something that respects the natural cadence of a person's voice, regardless of pauses or blocks. WiM reconstructs the transcript locally, removing repetitions and formatting the text cleanly, so the user can communicate exactly what they intend to, completely stress-free."

Beyond accessibility, WiM is a step forward for digital privacy. The default Layer 1 transcription runs fully on-device — audio and transcripts never leave the phone. Users who want cloud-based reconstruction (Layer 2 and above) opt in explicitly via the in-app layer selector; selecting cloud layers sends audio to OpenAI for transcription and to OpenAI or Anthropic for reconstruction. This makes WiM useful for people with speech disfluencies, professionals handling sensitive information, privacy-conscious users, and users in areas with poor internet connectivity. The app features a floating bubble that lets users dictate directly into their favorite messaging, email, or notes applications.

WiM launches in English with additional input languages on the roadmap. The app is available on the Google Play Store. It is free to download for offline Layer 1 transcription; WiM Cloud costs $5.99/month and includes 300 cloud dictations, including up to 20 Layer IV dictations.

**About WiM**
WiM is an independent software project dedicated to creating accessible, privacy-first communication tools. Focused on empowering users through on-device processing and thoughtful design, WiM aims to make technology adapt to the human voice, not the other way around.
